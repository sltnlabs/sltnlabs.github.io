WHO SHOOTS AT THE LEFT BASKET IN THE FIRST HALF?

Left and right as you see the court from where you film. Teams swap baskets at half time.

  1. Film in landscape. Follow the play, but try to keep the rim in the picture when a shot goes up.
  2. Tap each rim once at the start. The app keeps track of it as you move.
  3. Made baskets count as 2. Tap 3 or 1 to fix the last one. Misses can be added with the + buttons.

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
