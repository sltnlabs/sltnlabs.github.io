Rookie Track privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Rookie Track is an app for parents to track their child's basketball games. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. Rookie Track has no account, no server and no analytics. SLTN Labs (the developer) cannot see your data.

What the app stores

  • About the player you track: first name, jersey number, age group, team (optional) and a photo (optional).
  • About each game: the opponent's name, the date, the plays you log (shots, rebounds, assists and so on), where shots were taken if you mark them, minutes on court and the final score if you enter it.

Where it is stored

Only on your device, in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. If your phone backs up to iCloud or your computer, the backup may include this data; those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's terms.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It contains no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

Camera and photos

The app asks for access only when you choose to add a player photo. The photo is cropped, made smaller and stored inside the app on your phone. It is never uploaded.

Sharing

Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share a game summary, for example with a coach. You pick the app and the person to send it to.

Children

The app is used by parents. A parent enters the information about their child and keeps it on their own phone. We do not collect any information from or about children.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single game from its report screen.
  • Change or remove the player details and photo with Edit on the home screen.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off the phone, such as backup or sharing with a coach online, we will update this policy and ask for your agreement in the app before anything is sent.
