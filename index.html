How we build

Three rules, every app.

01

Useful first

Each app solves one real problem well, then grows from what people actually use, not from a feature checklist.

02

Private by design

No accounts unless they’re needed, no ad networks, no selling data. If something leaves your phone, you chose it.

03

Built to last

Designed with an architect’s eye: simple foundations, careful upgrades, and your data kept safe from one version to the next.
