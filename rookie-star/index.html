How it works

Ready before warm-ups end.

Start a game

Your kid’s name, the opponent and team colours. Ten seconds, and it remembers them next time.

Tap as it happens

One tap for a basket, a rebound or a steal. Made a mistake? Undo, or fix any play later.

Read the report

The full game the moment it ends, then season trends as the games add up. Share it with a screenshot.
